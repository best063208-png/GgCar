## Brand & Style

This design system targets modern electric vehicle (EV) enterprise dealerships and sales consultants representing cutting-edge automotive brands such as DEEPAL and CHANGAN. The interface addresses high-velocity enterprise quoting, structured vehicle specification comparisons, test drive scheduling, and complex automotive financing structures (e.g., balloon payments, residual values, trade-in equity, insurance tiers, and government EV subsidies in Thailand).

The visual personality combines technical precision, automotive craftsmanship, and executive clarity. It balances dense, transaction-oriented financial tooling with the sleek, aerodynamic excitement associated with modern intelligent mobility. The emotional tone is authoritative, highly responsive, transparent, and confidence-inspiring‚Äîenabling automotive sales representatives to configure custom vehicle quotes alongside customers on tablets or desktop workstations with zero ambiguity.

The design philosophy blends **High-Precision Enterprise Architecture** with **Sleek Tech-Corporate** aesthetics:
- **Clean Structure:** Utilitarian, multi-column density framed by fine-line architectural borders.
- **Electric Accents:** Deliberate, high-energy cyan and electric teal accents evoking battery electrification, telemetry, and smart vehicle intelligence.
- **Data Clarity:** Uncompromising contrast ratios, structured micro-badges, tabular numeric alignment, and immediate calculated feedback states.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive, automotive-grade hierarchy where dark slate navy anchors the structural identity, while luminous cyan directs user interaction and emerald highlights financial advantages.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary Navy (`#0F172A`):** The core corporate identity color. Used for dominant top navigation, header containers, high-emphasis text, primary action buttons, active sidebar states, and major card headings.
- **Secondary Cyan / Electric Blue (`#0EA5E9` / `#06B6D4`):** Represents electrification and dynamic digital workflows. Applied to interactive focus rings, active step wizard bars, primary selection borders (e.g., chosen trim or battery pack), financial sliders, active toggle states, and key interactive metrics.
- **Tertiary Emerald (`#10B981`):** Reserved strictly for positive financial delta‚Äîsuch as government EV tax subsidies, promotional discount tags, zero-percent interest incentives, fuel/energy savings indicators, and customer trade-in credit adjustments.
- **Neutral Framework:**
  - Canvas Background: `#F8FAFC` provides an ultra-clean, glare-free working surface.
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` for primary cards and configuration sheets.
  - Sub-surfaces & Inactive Wells: `#F1F5F9` for read-only quote summaries, specification matrices, and input backgrounds.
  - Borders & Grid Lines: `#E2E8F0` for interior division lines; `#CBD5E1` for actionable component borders.
  - Body & Auxiliary Text: `#334155` for high-legibility body copy and `#64748B` for secondary labels, metadata, and currency markers (‡∏ø).

### Functional Color Guidelines
Avoid large flood fills of secondary cyan; reserve it for active paths, telemetry markers, and focused data points. Never use green for decorative elements‚Äîit must solely denote economic benefit, approved credit status, or positive margin calculations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a high-density, multi-pane enterprise structure engineered for simultaneous vehicle visualizer tracking, real-time recalculation of down payments, and itemized accessory inclusions.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (‚â• 1280px):** 12-column asymmetric grid. Standard split utilizes a 7-column configuration workspace (model selector, color picker, accessory check-matrix) alongside a 5-column sticky financial calculation sidebar (down payment slider, loan term tenure, monthly repayment, down payment subtotal, insurance tiers, and PDF quote generator CTA).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Sales representatives use full-width step wizards with collapsible drawer panels for the financial breakdown sheet.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Critical monthly calculation anchors to a persistent sticky bottom utility bar showing the monthly installment and the "Send Official Quote" primary CTA.
- **Spacing Density:** 
  - `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern micro-interactions, badge padding, and field label offsets.
  - `space-md` (16px) establishes internal card padding and input field grouping.
  - `space-lg` (24px) separates major vehicle configuration modules (e.g., powertrain selection vs. interior package).
  - `space-xl` (32px) separates primary architectural sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **low-contrast architectural outlines combined with cool ambient shadows**, keeping the workspace structured, sharp, and data-dense without visual clutter.

### Hierarchy Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Completely flat base workspace.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Worksheets):** `#FFFFFF` paired with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Tools & Sticky Calculation Bars):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with `#CBD5E1` and elevated using `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Customer Credit Overlays, EV Subsidies Details):** Centered surfaces floating over `#0F172A` with a 40% alpha backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm`), casting `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Active Selection Depth:** Selected vehicle trims, rim options, or payment plans do not rely on elevation jumps. Instead, they activate a 2px crisp border of `#0EA5E9` alongside a very faint internal tint (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.04)`).
